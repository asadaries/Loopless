Loopless

Two one-time steps, then the toolbar button is all you need.

1. Camera and microphone

Chrome won’t show a permission prompt from the invisible page that does the recording, so the access has to be granted here first. It’s remembered for good.

2. Google Drive

Optional. Without it you can still record and download; with it, takes upload to a “Loopless recordings” folder. Needs an OAuth client ID in manifest.json — the README walks through it.

Good to know

  1. In “Screen + camera”, your camera is drawn into the video as a circle at the bottom-left.
  2. Recording keeps running when the popup closes, you switch tabs, or you minimise Chrome.
  3. Alt+Shift+R starts and stops without opening the popup. Change it at chrome://extensions/shortcuts.
  4. Chrome blocks recording of chrome:// pages and the Web Store.
